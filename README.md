Career selection is an important decision that can significantly influence an individual's
future. Traditional career guidance methods often rely on questionnaires that may not fully
engage users. This project presents CAMINO, a gamified career guidance system designed
to assess users through interactive mini games based on the RIASEC career interest model
and the OCEAN personality framework. The system analyzes user performance during
gameplay to identify interests and personality traits, then generates personalized career
recommendations. By combining assessment and entertainment, CAMINO provides a more
engaging and effective alternative to conventional career guidance approaches. The project
demonstrates the potential of gamification in supporting self-discovery and informed career
decision-making.
